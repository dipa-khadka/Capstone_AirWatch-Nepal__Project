What is AQI?

AQI (Air Quality Index) is one number that tells how clean the air is. 0 is very clean. The higher the number, the worse the air. Each colour below is a health level.

Trends

How did the air change over the last days? Each dot is the average AQI of one day, and the dot colour is its health level. The dashed line is the average of the whole period. Pick a ready range or choose your own dates.

The coloured background shows the health level of each AQI range. Empty gaps in the line mean there was no data on those days.
